One forecast for each set of rules

The firecracker rules for Delhi in 2026 had not been announced when we locked the forecast, so we give one forecast per regime. Each is the average of past Diwalis under the same rules, with an 80% prediction interval.

Exactly what is being forecast

Quantity. mean hourly (observed - BAU) PM2.5, 18:00 8 Nov to 02:59 9 Nov 2026 IST, missing hours carried forward from the last valid hour.

Where. CPCB Delhi network median, hours with 3 or more reporting sites (config/stations.csv).

Baseline. LightGBM on ERA5 weather, calendar and wind-aware fire index; Diwali windows excluded from training; out-of-fold prediction by ISO week (src/elevenwinters/analysis/partb.py).

Scoring. After 8 Nov 2026 the same code is rerun with data through 15 Nov 2026. The forecast for the regime actually in force counts as correct if the observed excess falls inside its interval.
